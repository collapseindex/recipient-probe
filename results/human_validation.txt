=== Human validation of the 'honoring' behavior measure ===
Blind subset: 60 replies (24 recognize-default + 24 recognize-steered + 12 evaluate anchors), Qwen-3B, L30.
One annotator, shown only sender_message + assistant_reply (condition + auto-label hidden), marked
offers_feedback y/n. Files: ratings_blind_filled.csv (human), ratings_key.csv (auto labels + condition).

Agreement (human vs automated):
  human vs LEXICON  : accuracy 0.867, Cohen's kappa 0.693  (substantial)
  human vs EMBEDDING: accuracy 0.817, Cohen's kappa 0.540  (moderate)
  lexicon vs embedding: accuracy 0.783, kappa 0.496

Attention check: evaluate anchors (sender ASKED for critique) -> human marked feedback 11/12 = 0.92. Engaged.

Human independently reproduces the steering effect (honoring = NOT offering feedback, by human label):
  HUMAN honoring on recognize: default 0.75 -> steered 1.00
  LEXICON honoring on recognize: default 0.58 -> steered 0.96   (same direction/magnitude)

Read: the lexicon is a substantial proxy for human judgment (kappa 0.69), the human is slightly more lenient
(marks fewer replies as feedback -> higher default honoring 0.75 vs lexicon 0.58), but BOTH show the recovery.
The behavioral 'honoring' numbers are not an artifact of automated scoring. Folded into Sec. Discards +
Limitations. One annotator; a multi-annotator study would tighten it further.
