A third intent axis with a CLEAN (low-BoW) design, so the represent-then-lag
phenomenon is not all one contrast (recognize/evaluate) or riding a lexical crutch (the old vent/solve axis was

over a neutral surface-matched core; frames lexically diverse within each class so leave-one-frame-out defeats BoW.

READING:
- REPRESENTS (headline, airtight): probe 0.71-0.86 vs BoW 0.57 on all six models / four families. A genuinely
  different, fully-inferred, BoW-clean intent is represented everywhere. The representation generalizes across
  intents, not just across models, and not via lexical shortcut.
- DISCARD (modest, on-thesis): help-intent draws solutions more than support-intent in EVERY model (help_sol >
  sup_sol, gaps 0.05-0.25), so intent modulates behavior; but the models solutionize weakly on either (this
  intent is fully inferred, no stated request), so the behavioral separation is smaller than the primary axis.
- STEER/RECOVER: steering toward support DOES lift honoring to 0.85-1.00, BUT it FAILS the specificity control
  (modal_axis3_spec.py, sweep_axis3_spec.json): random matched-norm directions move solutionizing AS MUCH as the
  learned direction. S_true=6 (Qwen-3B), 11 (Phi-3.5) vs random max|S|=19, 16; p_rand=0.31, 0.14 (NOT sig);
  shuffled max=11,11, p_shuf=0.46,0.15. So the axis3 behavioral "recovery" is NOT specific to the intent
  direction -- it is a generic large-perturbation effect on a weak, low-rate behavior. We do NOT claim a causal
  handle on this axis. (The primary axis PASSED specificity at p=0.02; this one does not.)

BOTTOM LINE (revised after the spec control): the third axis establishes that the REPRESENTATION generalizes to a
clean, genuinely different intent (probe 0.71-0.86 vs BoW 0.57, all six) -- the strong claim, airtight. The
BEHAVIORAL chain is weak: the readout tracks the intent only faintly (help>support solutions by 0.05-0.25) and
steering the direction is NOT a specific handle (fails random-null). Scope the contribution to the representation,
report the steering as a null. This is the expected shape (represented strongly, acted on too weakly to steer
specifically) and it goes on record as another null-against-ourselves.
