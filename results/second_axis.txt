- RECOVER: steering toward-vent recovers listening -- Qwen 9/12->12/12 honored (advice 12->1),

  coherence-limited (baseline already solutionizes), so its recovery shows on the honoring axis.
- Classifier audited: advice-language -> solve, empathetic listening (incl. "can you tell me more?") -> vent.
